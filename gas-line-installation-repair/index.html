Safety First

If You Smell Gas, Do This

Leave Immediately

Get everyone out of the house right away, without stopping to open windows or investigate.

Don't Operate Anything Electrical

Don't flip light switches, use a phone inside, or start a vehicle in an attached garage, any spark is a real risk.

Call From Outside

Call the gas utility's emergency line and then us once you're a safe distance away.

Don't Try To Find Or Fix It Yourself

Leave locating and repairing the leak to trained technicians with the right detection equipment.

Why It Happens

Gas Line Installation & Repair In Melbourne

Many Melbourne homes near downtown and Eau Gallie were built with gas service for water heating or cooking decades ago. Older black iron gas piping can develop corrosion at threaded joints over that span of time, which is the most common source of a slow leak we're called to investigate. A joint that was properly sealed decades ago can still degrade gradually as the pipe dope or tape used at the time breaks down.

Newer construction closer to the Palm Bay line more often uses corrugated stainless steel tubing for gas runs, which resists corrosion better but still requires correct bonding and support to meet code, something we verify on every new installation. A CSST run that wasn't properly bonded during the original installation is a real code and safety issue we check for on any inspection.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Rotten Egg Smell

The distinctive sulfur odor added to natural gas specifically so a leak is noticeable; treat this as an emergency, always.

Hissing Sound Near A Line Or Appliance

An audible hiss near a gas connection, even without a noticeable smell.

Dead Or Discolored Vegetation

Vegetation dying above a buried gas line's path can indicate an underground leak.

Pilot Light Won't Stay Lit

On a gas appliance, this can be a sign of a supply issue rather than the appliance itself, worth ruling out.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

For a suspected leak, we use a combustible gas detector to trace the source before touching anything. We never assume the leak's location from a smell alone since gas migrates through soil and wall cavities. For a new installation, we size the line correctly for the appliance's BTU demand and pressure-test every joint before connecting any appliance.

Equipment We Use

  • Combustible gas leak detector.
  • Line pressure testing equipment.
  • Appliance BTU demand calculation.

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I do if I smell gas in my house?

Leave the house immediately without operating any switches or appliances, call the gas utility's emergency line from a safe distance outside, and then call us. Never try to locate or fix a suspected leak yourself.

Do you install gas lines for outdoor grills and fire features?

Yes, we run and connect new gas lines for outdoor kitchens, grills, and fire features, sized correctly for the appliance and installed to code.

Is it normal for older Melbourne homes to still have black iron gas pipe?

Yes, it's common in homes built before more recent code updates. Black iron itself isn't inherently a problem, but corroded threaded joints on an older run are the most frequent source of a slow leak, which is why we inspect joint condition closely on any older system.

Do you handle the permit for a new gas line installation?

Yes, we handle the permit application and coordinate the required inspection through the City of Melbourne Building Department as part of the installation, so you don't have to manage that process yourself.

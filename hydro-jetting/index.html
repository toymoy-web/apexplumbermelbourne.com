Hydro Jetting in Melbourne, FL

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water, not a cable, to scour the full interior of a drain or sewer line, cutting through root intrusion, grease buildup, and mineral scale that a snake only punches a channel through. In Melbourne, where mature landscaping near older neighborhoods sends roots into aging clay and cast-iron laterals, jetting is often the difference between a line that stays clear and one that backs up again within weeks of cabling.

Not Sure What's Wrong?

Snaked It And It Still Clogs A Few Weeks Later?

A common pattern we hear: a line was snaked, cleared for a short while, then clogged again, or the cable got stuck at some distance down the line and couldn't push through. Both usually mean the problem is bigger than a simple blockage, most often root intrusion at a pipe joint that a cable can push past but never actually clears. Hydro jetting scours the full pipe wall instead of just punching through the center.

Why It Happens

Hydro Jetting In Melbourne

Melbourne's older neighborhoods near downtown and Eau Gallie combine mature trees with aging clay and cast-iron sewer laterals. Roots seeking moisture at a pipe joint are one of the most common reasons a line clogs repeatedly instead of once. A tree planted decades ago near the original lateral run is often the exact cause, even when the root system isn't visible anywhere above ground.

Hard water from the Floridan Aquifer also plays a role inside drain and sewer pipes over time, leaving mineral scale that narrows the interior diameter and gives grease and debris something to catch on, which jetting clears far more completely than cabling. Kitchen lines in particular accumulate this combination of scale and grease faster than bathroom lines do.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Recurring Clogs

The same line clogging again within weeks of being cabled clear.

Cable Gets Stuck

A drain snake reaching a point it can't push past, often a sign of root intrusion or a pipe offset.

Multiple Slow Drains

Several fixtures draining slowly at once, suggesting the main line itself is restricted.

Foul Odor From Drains

A persistent sewer smell that cabling alone hasn't resolved.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

We run a sewer camera through the line before jetting, not after, so we know exactly what we're dealing with and whether the pipe's condition can safely handle jetting's water pressure. A structurally sound line with root intrusion or scale buildup is a good jetting candidate; a line with a cracked or collapsing section needs repair first, since jetting a compromised pipe can make the damage worse.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older Melbourne homes with clay or cast-iron pipe?

We check the pipe's condition with a camera first. Jetting is safe for most clay and cast-iron lines in sound structural condition; a fragile or already-cracked line calls for repair before jetting, which we'll explain if that's the case.

How is this different from regular drain cleaning?

Standard drain cleaning cables through a blockage. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire interior wall of the pipe, which clears root intrusion and scale far more completely.

Why did my line clog again right after being snaked?

A cable typically punches a channel through the clog rather than clearing the full pipe diameter, so root intrusion or heavy scale returns quickly. A camera inspection shows whether jetting is the better fix.

Do you jet residential lines or just commercial?

Both. We jet residential kitchen and main sewer lines across Melbourne as often as commercial grease-line jobs.

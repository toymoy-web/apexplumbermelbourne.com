Water Heater Installation & Replacement in Melbourne, FL

Water heater installation and replacement covers swapping a failing tank for a new one or moving up to a tankless unit, sized and installed to Melbourne's real conditions. Because municipal water here runs hard, drawn from the Floridan Aquifer and Lake Washington, water heaters in this market tend to scale up and fail sooner than the same unit would in a softer-water city. Why It Happens

Water Heater Installation & Replacement In Melbourne

Melbourne's water hardness is well-documented by independent testing of the city's Floridan Aquifer and Lake Washington supply, and mineral scale is the single biggest factor shortening water heater lifespan here. Sediment builds up inside the tank, which forces the heating element to work harder and eventually causes the popping or rumbling sound that usually means a unit is near the end of its life.

Homes closer to the Indian River Lagoon and older construction near downtown and Eau Gallie sometimes still run water heaters on original 1980s-1990s plumbing runs, which affects venting and connection options when it's time to replace the unit, especially when moving from tank to tankless. Newer construction closer to the Palm Bay line typically has more flexible venting already in place, which usually keeps a tank-to-tankless conversion simpler and faster.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

No Hot Water

Cold showers, or hot water that runs out much faster than it used to.

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Water coming out with a rust tint, often a sign of internal tank corrosion.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup rumbling against the tank wall as the unit heats, common with Melbourne's hard water.

Age And Visible Leaks

A tank over 8-10 years old, or moisture pooling around its base.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

Replacement starts with checking the tank, venting, and connections, since Melbourne's mix of older and newer housing stock means the right replacement path isn't always the same house to house. We confirm whether the existing gas or electric connection and venting can support a straight swap, or whether moving to a tankless unit needs additional venting or an electrical upgrade, before quoting a price. Every installation is completed to Florida Building Code plumbing and mechanical provisions, with permits and inspections handled through the City of Melbourne Building Department where required.

Repair Options

What Fixing It Actually Involves

Traditional Tank

Lower upfront cost, simpler installation, typically a 10-15 year lifespan even with hard-water wear.

Tankless

Continuous hot water on demand, compact wall-mounted footprint, and a lifespan that can reach 20 years with the right maintenance schedule against scale buildup.

Haul-Away Included

The old unit is removed and disposed of as part of every installation, no extra call needed.

Comparison

Tank vs. Tankless For A Melbourne Home

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront equipment and installation cost.
  • Simpler installation on existing gas or electric connections.
  • Hot water supply is limited by tank size.
  • Sediment from hard water settles at the bottom over time.

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water with no tank to run out.
  • Compact wall-mounted unit frees up closet or garage space.
  • Lower standby energy cost since it only heats water on demand.
  • Needs a periodic descaling flush given Melbourne's hard water.

FAQs

Common Questions

How fast can you install a replacement in Melbourne?

Most straight tank-to-tank replacements are completed same-day for calls before early afternoon. A tank-to-tankless conversion needing new venting may take a bit longer.

Does Melbourne's hard water mean I should get a water softener too?

It's worth discussing. A softener reduces the scale buildup that shortens water heater life here, though it's a separate system from the water heater itself and not required for installation.

Do you haul away my old water heater?

Yes, removal and disposal of the old unit is included in every replacement, no separate charge.

Is the price locked in before you start?

Yes. We inspect the tank, venting, and connections first, then give you a flat price you approve before any installation work begins.

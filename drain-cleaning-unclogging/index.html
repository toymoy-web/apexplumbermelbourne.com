Why It Happens

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging In Melbourne

Older homes near downtown Melbourne and the Eau Gallie area often still run clay or cast-iron sewer laterals. Mature landscaping across these established neighborhoods means root intrusion is a real, recurring cause of a main line that clogs repeatedly instead of just once.

Melbourne's hard water, drawn from Floridan Aquifer wells, leaves mineral scale inside drain and sewer pipes over time, which narrows the interior of the line and makes it catch grease, hair, and debris faster than it would in a home on softer water.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Slow-Draining Fixtures

Multiple sinks, tubs, or the shower all draining slower than usual, often a sign the problem is further down the line, not just one fixture.

Gurgling Sounds

A gurgling noise from a drain or toilet when another fixture runs, which points to a blockage affecting the shared line.

Water Backing Up

Water rising in a tub or floor drain when the washing machine runs or a toilet flushes.

Recurring Clogs

The same drain clogging again within weeks of a store-bought snake or chemical cleaner, a sign the real cause wasn't cleared.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

For a single slow drain, we start with a drain snake to physically clear the blockage. For a main line, a sewer camera goes in first to show exactly what we're dealing with, a grease clog, a collapsed section, or root intrusion at a joint, instead of guessing. Once the camera confirms the cause, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full interior of the pipe, which clears root intrusion and built-up scale far more completely than cabling alone.

Comparison

Store-Bought Snake vs. Professional Clearing

Store-Bought Snake Or Chemical Cleaner

  • Punches a hole through the clog rather than clearing the full pipe diameter.
  • No way to see what's actually causing the blockage.
  • Chemical drain cleaners can damage older cast-iron or PVC joints.
  • Root intrusion and scale buildup return within weeks.

Camera-Confirmed Professional Clearing

  • Sewer camera shows the actual cause before any equipment goes in.
  • Hydro jetting clears the full interior, not just a channel through the clog.
  • Identifies a cracked or collapsed section before it becomes a bigger repair.
  • Written diagnosis if the issue is tied to a warranty or insurance claim.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning at night or on weekends?

Yes, our emergency line for Melbourne is answered 24 hours a day, including weekends, holidays, and during active storm events.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

We check the pipe material and condition with a camera first. Hydro jetting is safe for most PVC and cast-iron lines in good structural condition; a fragile or already-cracked line calls for a different approach, which we'll explain before starting.

Why does my drain keep clogging after I've snaked it myself?

A store-bought snake usually punches a hole through the blockage rather than clearing the full diameter of the pipe, so root intrusion or heavy grease buildup returns quickly. A camera inspection shows whether hydro jetting or a spot repair is the more complete fix.

Does hurricane season make sewer backups worse in Melbourne?

Yes. Heavy rainfall in low-lying sections near the Indian River Lagoon can push the sewer system toward capacity, which is when we see the most main-line backup calls, typically June through November.

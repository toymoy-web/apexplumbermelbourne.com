Commercial plumbing covers the systems a Melbourne business depends on that a residential call doesn't: grease trap maintenance for restaurants, backflow testing for cross-connection compliance, higher-capacity water heaters, and multi-fixture drain systems. We treat commercial downtime differently from a residential repair, since a closed restroom or a backed-up kitchen line directly affects a business's ability to operate, and we scope every commercial job with that urgency in mind rather than treating it as a larger version of a residential call requiring the same approach.

Why It Happens

Commercial Plumbing In Melbourne

Melbourne's restaurant and retail corridor along Wickham Road and the Eau Gallie Arts District generates real, recurring commercial grease trap and drain-line demand. Commercial kitchens produce far more fats, oils, and grease than a residential kitchen ever does. Without a regular pumping schedule, a grease trap that's working fine one month can back up the next as accumulated grease finally restricts flow, often at the worst possible time during a busy service.

Multi-unit commercial and retail properties across Melbourne also fall under the same backflow testing requirements as residential irrigation customers, but often with larger, higher-capacity devices that need a certified tester experienced with commercial-scale equipment. Property managers overseeing several units at once often coordinate multiple devices' testing on the same visit to keep everything on a consistent annual schedule.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Grease Trap Backing Up

Slow drains or odor near a commercial kitchen's grease trap, a sign it needs pumping or servicing.

Multiple Fixtures Affected At Once

Several restrooms or fixtures backing up together, pointing to a main line issue rather than one fixture.

Water Heater Can't Keep Up With Demand

A commercial water heater running out of hot water during peak business hours.

Backflow Device Test Overdue

A commercial backflow preventer past its annual testing and reporting requirement.

Recurring Restroom Clogs Across A Multi-Unit Property

Multiple tenant units reporting similar drain issues, often pointing to a shared main line problem rather than separate isolated clogs.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you work with property managers rather than just the business owner directly?

Yes, we regularly work directly with property managers and landlords on behalf of tenants for commercial and multi-unit properties across Melbourne, and can coordinate access and billing however works best for your arrangement.

Can you schedule around our business hours?

In most cases, yes. We'll discuss your operating hours and schedule non-emergency work to minimize disruption wherever the repair allows it, including after-hours or early-morning windows for businesses that can't have plumbing work happening during service hours.

How often does a restaurant grease trap need service?

It depends on kitchen volume, but most commercial kitchens need grease trap pumping on a regular schedule, which we can help establish based on your actual usage rather than a generic interval that doesn't match your kitchen's real output.

Do you offer emergency service for commercial properties?

Yes, our emergency line covers commercial properties the same as residential, 24 hours a day, since we know a shut restroom or an active leak during business hours has a real, direct cost for a business.

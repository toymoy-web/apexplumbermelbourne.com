Shower & Bathtub Installation in Melbourne, FL

Shower and bathtub installation covers replacing an aging fixture, converting a tub to a walk-in shower, or plumbing a new bathroom addition. In Melbourne, this work often means updating supply and drain connections that were originally sized for a much older fixture, since a tub-to-shower conversion or a modern multi-head shower valve has different rough-in requirements than what a 1980s or 1990s bathroom was built around. We inspect the existing plumbing before quoting the job so the scope reflects what's actually behind the wall, not just what's visible.

Why It Happens

Shower & Bathtub Installation In Melbourne

Homes near downtown Melbourne and Eau Gallie built decades ago often have original single-handle or two-handle shower valves that don't meet current anti-scald code requirements. We address this as part of any full installation rather than reusing an outdated valve. An anti-scald valve holds water temperature steady even if another fixture in the house draws water suddenly, which older valves simply aren't designed to do.

Melbourne's hard water also affects tile grout lines and fixture finishes over time, which is worth factoring into material choices during a remodel, since some finishes hold up to mineral buildup better than others. A finish that resists mineral spotting can meaningfully cut down on the cleaning a hard-water household otherwise has to do to keep a new fixture looking new.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Outdated Or Leaking Valve

An old shower valve that doesn't hold temperature steadily or drips between uses.

Cracked Or Failing Tub Surface

Visible cracks or a worn surface in an older tub that's better replaced than repeatedly patched.

Wanting A Tub-To-Shower Conversion

A household looking to convert an underused tub into a walk-in shower for accessibility or space.

Bathroom Renovation Or Addition

New construction or a full remodel requiring fresh supply and drain rough-in for the fixture.

Inconsistent Water Temperature

Temperature swinging noticeably when another fixture in the house runs, a sign the existing valve isn't pressure-balanced.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

We inspect the existing rough-in, supply lines, drain location, and valve type before quoting an installation, since a straight fixture swap and a full tub-to-shower conversion have very different scopes. For a conversion, we also confirm the drain location works for the new pan or base, or plan the necessary relocation.

Repair Options

What Fixing It Actually Involves

Direct Fixture Replacement

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you convert my bathtub to a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are a common project in Melbourne, and we handle the drain relocation, valve upgrade, and full installation as one job, coordinated so you know each step before it happens.

Do I need to replace my shower valve if I'm just swapping the tub?

Not always, but if the existing valve is original and doesn't meet current anti-scald requirements, we'll flag that during inspection and explain why upgrading it is worth doing at the same time, rather than leaving an outdated valve behind a brand-new fixture.

How long does a shower or tub installation take?

A direct fixture replacement is often completed within a day; a full tub-to-shower conversion with drain relocation typically takes longer, which we'll scope specifically for your bathroom before starting, including a clear timeline so you know when the bathroom will be back in service.

Do you handle the tile and surround, or just the plumbing?

We handle the plumbing rough-in, valve, and fixture connections. Tile and surround finishing is typically coordinated with your contractor or finish trade unless discussed otherwise upfront, and we're glad to work alongside whoever's handling that portion of the project.

Do you cover South Patrick Shores and Tortoise Island, ZIP 32937?

Yes, all of Satellite Beach ZIP 32937, including South Patrick Shores and Tortoise Island, is inside our core service area.

Why does Satellite Beach seem to have a lot of similar-age plumbing problems?

The city was built almost entirely in a single wave from 1957 through the 1970s for the aerospace workforce. Many homes' original copper supply lines and sewer laterals are reaching the end of typical service life around the same time, which is a real, predictable pattern rather than coincidence.

Do canal-front homes need different plumbing service?

The plumbing itself is standard, but canal-front lots like those on Tortoise Island sometimes have supply lines routed closer to brackish water, which we account for during leak detection and any excavation work.

Can you coordinate access near Patrick Space Force Base?

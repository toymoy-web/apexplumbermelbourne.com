Think It's Time For A Cleaning, Or Been A Few Years?

Homeowners often reach out not because something's obviously broken, but because it's been a while since the unit was serviced, or because they've noticed reduced hot water flow and suspect it needs a flush. Why It Happens

Tankless Water Heater Service In Melbourne

Melbourne's water, drawn from Floridan Aquifer wells and blended with Lake Washington surface water, carries enough mineral content that a tankless heat exchanger scales up meaningfully faster here than in a softer-water market. Manufacturers typically recommend an annual flush; in Melbourne, units without a whole-home softener often need it sooner.

Homes without a dedicated water softener see the fastest scale buildup, since nothing upstream is reducing the mineral load before it reaches the unit's internal heat exchanger coils.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Error Code On Display

Most tankless units show a specific error code before failing outright, which we can look up and diagnose over the phone.

Reduced Hot Water Flow

Water not reaching full temperature or flow rate dropping under normal demand, a classic scale symptom.

Longer Wait For Hot Water

A noticeably longer delay before hot water reaches the fixture.

Unit Cycling On And Off

Short-cycling rather than maintaining steady output, often tied to a scaled or failing flow sensor.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

We start by pulling the unit's error code history where available, which usually narrows the problem to ignition, flow sensor, or heat exchanger scale before we open anything up. A descaling flush uses a pump and a mild descaling solution circulated through the heat exchanger, which is different equipment and a different process than anything used on a tank-style unit.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be serviced in Melbourne?

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling; given how hard Melbourne's water runs, a unit without a softener upstream often benefits from servicing closer to every 6-9 months.

What does an error code actually mean?

Tankless units display a specific code tied to the failure type, ignition, flow, or temperature sensing. We look up the code for your unit's brand before diagnosing further, rather than guessing.

Can a descaling flush fix reduced hot water flow?

In most cases where the cause is scale buildup, yes. If flow doesn't improve after a flush, that points to a specific component like the flow sensor rather than scale.

Would a water softener reduce how often I need service?

Yes. A whole-home softener removes much of the mineral load before it ever reaches the unit, which meaningfully extends the interval between descaling services.

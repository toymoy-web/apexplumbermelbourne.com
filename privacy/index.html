Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026.

Information We Collect

When you call Melbourne Apex Plumbing, your call may be recorded or tracked for quality assurance and scheduling purposes through our call tracking service. When you email us or reach out through a listed contact method, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address, and a description of your plumbing issue, solely to respond to your inquiry and provide service.

This website does not use a contact form, and we do not collect information through the site itself beyond standard, non-identifying server logs common to any website, such as which pages were visited.

How We Use Information

Information collected through a phone call or email is used to schedule and provide plumbing service, communicate with you about that service, and maintain accurate business records. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

We may share information with subcontractors or service partners directly involved in fulfilling your service request, such as a parts supplier, and as required by law.

Data Retention

We retain customer and service records for as long as reasonably necessary for business, tax, and legal purposes.

Your Choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by contacting us directly at contact@apexplumbermelbourne.com or (321) 463-9969.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to Melbourne Apex Plumbing at (321) 463-9969 or contact@apexplumbermelbourne.com.

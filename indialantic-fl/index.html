FAQs

Questions From Indialantic

Do you respond to Indialantic during hurricane season?

Yes, our emergency line stays open throughout hurricane season, and barrier-island calls get the same 24/7 dispatch as the mainland, weather permitting for safe travel across the causeway, and we'll always tell you honestly if conditions are delaying response.

Do you cover all of Indialantic, ZIP 32903?

Yes, all of Indialantic ZIP 32903 is inside our core service area, including the barrier island's full length from the boardwalk to the Melbourne Beach line.

Does salt air really affect indoor plumbing, not just outdoor fixtures?

It's most pronounced on exposed outdoor fixtures like hose bibs, but the humidity and salt content in coastal air can also accelerate corrosion on any exposed metal supply lines in garages or utility areas.

Are Indialantic condos along A1A serviced differently than single-family homes?

The individual unit's plumbing is diagnosed the same way, but a condo's shared supply or drain line sometimes involves coordinating with an HOA or property manager, which we're glad to handle directly rather than leaving that coordination to you.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Indialantic?

Indialantic is a small, single-ZIP town, the entire barrier-island footprint, from the boardwalk at 5th Avenue down to the Melbourne Beach line, falls under ZIP 32903, and that's the ZIP our daily routes cover in full.

Is emergency service available in Indialantic?

Yes, our emergency line covers Indialantic 24 hours a day, every day, including hurricane season, with dispatch crossing the Melbourne Causeway the same way it does for any barrier-island call.

Why It Happens

Hose Bib & Outdoor Faucet Repair In Melbourne

Melbourne's outdoor-living climate means hose bibs run nearly year-round rather than seasonally. That puts more total open-close cycles on the internal washer and stem than the same fixture would see in a market with a real winter shutdown period. That constant use is the single biggest factor in how quickly a washer wears out here compared to a colder climate.

Salt air common in coastal Brevard County neighborhoods accelerates corrosion on exposed metal hose bib components faster than it would inland, which is a real factor in fixtures near Melbourne Beach, Indialantic, and Satellite Beach specifically. A brass hose bib that would last decades inland can show real corrosion within a much shorter span in a direct coastal-air location.

Signs To Watch For

Common Warning Signs

Dripping When Off

Water continuing to drip after the handle is fully closed, usually a worn washer or stem.

Leaking Where The Faucet Meets The Wall

Water at the wall penetration point, which can indicate a supply line issue behind the fixture rather than the faucet itself.

Corroded Or Stuck Handle

A handle that's become difficult to turn or shows visible corrosion, common in salt-air coastal areas.

Missing Or Failed Backflow Device

No visible anti-siphon vacuum breaker on the faucet, or one that's cracked or missing its check valve.

Low Flow At The Spigot

Reduced flow at an outdoor faucet while indoor fixtures are normal, sometimes a sign of internal mineral buildup or a partially closed shutoff.

How We Diagnose It

Finding The Real Cause

Most hose bib repairs are diagnosed by inspecting the washer, stem, and packing directly. For a leak at the wall penetration, we check whether the issue is the faucet's connection or the supply line behind it, since a wall-penetration leak sometimes indicates a bigger problem than the visible fixture alone.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why does my outdoor faucet need a backflow device?

Florida plumbing code requires an anti-siphon vacuum breaker on outdoor hose bibs to prevent contaminated water, from a garden hose sitting in a puddle or chemical sprayer, from siphoning back into your home's drinking water supply if pressure drops.

Do hose bibs freeze in Melbourne?

Freeze risk here is minimal compared to most of the country, but salt air and near-constant use cause more wear than cold-climate hose bibs typically see, which is the bigger factor in this market.

Should I upgrade to a frost-free hose bib?

It's optional given Melbourne's mild climate, but a frost-free style is also more resistant to the corrosion and wear pattern common here, so it's often a reasonable upgrade during a replacement rather than a strict necessity.

Can a leaking hose bib affect my water bill?

Yes, a continuous drip adds up over a billing cycle just like an indoor fixture leak would, and it's usually an inexpensive fix once diagnosed, so it's worth addressing as soon as you notice it rather than waiting.
